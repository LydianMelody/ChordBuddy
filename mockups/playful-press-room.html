How motion behaves

It answers youThings move when you click, hover or choose. Nothing moves on its own except the open song's reels, and only in Lively.
The playhead never bouncesThe chord that's playing moves exactly in time. Only the pop around it uses a spring.
Calm for showsCalm keeps quick click feedback and drops anything that loops. Still turns motion off. Your computer's reduce motion setting picks Calm for you.
Cheap to drawOnly movement and fading animate, so audio and the visualizer keep their speed.
