Press Room Round two · combined

Greenroom's layout, the Deck's paper chart and channel strips, and Riso's neo-brutalist ink, with three ink palettes to try. It's built around your notes:

  • Three chart modes: Navigate (click anything to jump there), Edit (fix chords and lyrics in place, with + to add a chord), Timing (the same chart turns true-to-time, so you drag any word to any beat, in any measure or line, and zoom with −/+ or Ctrl+scroll for precision)
  • Navigation bar modeled on your scrubbing example, with zoom −/+, a draggable overview, follow-playhead, and drag-to-loop with bar/beat snapping
  • Practice speed in 5% steps and a loop toggle; double-click a section block to loop it
  • No Original/Stems switch: songs that haven't been analyzed are locked until they're ready
  • The chart is a real letter-sized page, and Export PDF prints just that page

What every version keeps

  • One shared scrubber for Chart and Stems, with section blocks, waveform and a Bar · Beat readout
  • Repeats written inline right after the last chord, like F♯ (×2), never in section headers
  • Stacked lyric lines for repeated passes, with the current pass lit up as it plays
  • Click a chord to edit it, with This pass or All matching passes and Jump here
  • Seven stems with volume, mute and solo, plus Original/Stems and master volume
  • Set lists, song library with analysis status, Undo/Redo, saved state, Lead sheet and MIDI tabs
